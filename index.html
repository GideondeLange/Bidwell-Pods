Who We Are

Small footprint. Full-size standard.

Bidwell Pods designs and builds compact, steel-framed living spaces for people who need more room without a full house extension.

Every pod starts as living space, not a repurposed shell — insulated, wired and plumbed as a proper structure from the first sketch, then finished in warm timber and clean, considered interiors.

From a single glamping unit on a game farm to a fleet of site-accommodation pods for a construction crew, we build to one standard: durable, good-looking, and ready to use the day it arrives.
