Steel vs. Shipping Container

Why a purpose-built pod wins.

A pod is designed as living space from the first sketch — not adapted from a box built for freight.

FactorShipping Container ConversionBidwell Steel-Framed Pod
InsulationThin corrugated steel built to move cargo, not regulate temperature — runs hot in summer, cold on Highveld winter mornings without expensive retrofitting.Insulated from the start, for South African conditions year-round.
Layout FreedomDimensions fixed by international shipping standards.Designed around how the space will actually be used — window placement, ceiling height and room flow included.
AppearanceStill reads as a converted container.Timber finishes and a proper roofline add to a property's value and kerb appeal.
Structural IntegrityCutting window and door openings weakens the steel, often requiring reinforcement that erodes the cost advantage.Engineered as an open, livable structure from the outset.
Frequently Asked Questions

What clients ask before they build.

How much does a pod cost in South Africa?+
Bidwell pods start from R45,000 for a small backyard office or guard house, with self-contained living pods from R215,000 and our largest 3 Bedroom model from R1,095,000. Every pod is custom designed and sized to your site, and financing is available.
Do I need planning permission for a pod?+
It depends on your municipality, the pod's size, and whether it's a permanent or removable structure. Many secondary dwellings under a certain footprint qualify for simplified approval, but rules vary by council — we can guide you through what's typically required in your area before you build.
Are pods better than shipping container conversions?+
For most residential and hospitality uses, yes. Purpose-built steel-framed pods are designed around insulation, natural light and layout from the start, whereas container conversions have to work around a shipping container's original shape and thin steel walls.
Can a pod generate rental income?+
Yes. A self-contained backyard pod with its own entrance, bathroom and kitchenette can be let out long-term to a tenant or listed short-term on platforms like Airbnb, giving homeowners an additional income stream without the cost and disruption of a full home extension.
